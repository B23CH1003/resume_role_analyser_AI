# Resume Role Analyzer AI

## Overview

Resume Role Analyzer AI is an AI-powered career intelligence platform that evaluates resumes against specific job roles rather than providing generic ATS scores.

Users upload their resume and specify a target role such as Software Developer, Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist, or Quant Analyst. The system analyzes the resume, identifies skill gaps, project gaps, and generates a personalized roadmap for career improvement.

## Problem Statement

Most resume analyzers provide generic ATS scores without considering a candidate's target career path. As a result, candidates receive feedback that may not accurately reflect their suitability for a specific role.

A resume that performs well for Software Development may not be competitive for Machine Learning Engineering or Data Science roles.

## Solution

Resume Role Analyzer AI performs role-specific evaluation by comparing resume content against industry expectations and job requirements.

The platform focuses on:

* Skills
* Projects
* Coursework
* Tools & Technologies
* Experience

instead of resume formatting and design.

## What Makes It Different?

Unlike traditional ATS tools that focus primarily on resume formatting and keyword matching, Resume Role Analyzer AI evaluates role readiness.

The system helps users understand:

* How well they match a target role
* Which skills they are missing
* Which project categories they should build
* What actions will improve their profile the fastest
